Enterprise Refund Policy
Enterprise customers can request a refund within 45 days of purchase.

Premium Refund Policy
Premium customers can request a refund within 30 days of purchase.

Standard Refund Policy
Standard customers can request a refund within 15 days of purchase.

Password Policy
Employees must change their account password every 90 days.

Annual Leave Policy
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave each year.

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely for up to three days per week with manager approval.
